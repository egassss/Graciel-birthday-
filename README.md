# 🎂 Happy Birthday, Graciel! 💗🌸

A little birthday surprise made especially for **Graciel**. ✨

This is a cute interactive birthday website featuring:

- 🌸 Falling flower petals
- 💕 Animated heart effects
- 📸 A photo booth with special memories
- 🎂 A birthday cake scene
- 💌 A personal birthday letter
- 🎵 Background birthday music
- 🎥 A final video surprise
- ✨ Smooth transitions between each chapter

## 💗 The Surprise

The website takes Graciel through several little chapters:

**01 — Birthday Wish** 🌷  
A special birthday message.

**02 — Photo Booth** 📸  
A collection of favorite memories.

**03 — Make a Wish** 🎂  
A birthday cake and wishes.

**04 — From Me, To You** 💌  
A personal letter.

**05 — One Last Surprise** 🎥  
A final video made just for her.
